BNITZ abstract map decision notes

Worked examples 1–6
===================

The Platonic Representation Hypothesis
The literature survey and the authors’ comparison independently support the central hypothesis, so each gets its own Evidence planet.

Inference-Time Intervention
The intervention is the Nucleus, and the gain in Alpaca’s truthfulness from 32.5% to 65.1% measures how well it works, so that result is Evidence.

Progress Measures for Grokking
The Fourier ablations are Evidence for the Method because they test whether the network actually uses the algorithm the authors reconstructed.

Scaling Laws for Neural Language Models
The compute allocation recommendation follows from the measured scaling law, so it is an Implication.

Attention Is All You Need
Translation performance and generalization to parsing are two separate Evidence planets because they test distinct claims, and there is no Method planet because the abstract identifies attention as the basis of the Transformer without explaining how the architecture works.

Bitcoin
The honest CPU majority qualifies the chain-selection mechanism, so Scope attaches to Method.

Worked examples 7–12
====================

Collective Dynamics of ‘Small-World’ Networks
Real networks exhibiting the predicted topology provide Evidence, while faster spreading is an Implication of that topology.

On the (Im)possibility of Obfuscating Programs
The counterexample supports the impossibility theorem, and we mark this passage as Method because the authors use it to explain how they prove the theorem.

What Is the Sapir-Whorf Hypothesis?
The observed color effect and the proposed mechanism that explains it form the Nucleus, and the blocking experiment provides Evidence because the effect disappears as predicted.

The Geometry of Truth
Visualization reveals the linear pattern, transfer tests whether it generalizes, and intervention tests whether it influences model outputs, so each is a separate Evidence planet supporting the Nucleus.

Layers at Similar Depths Generate Similar Activations Across LLM Architectures
The two observations form a single Evidence planet because together they show that nearest-neighbor relationships change from layer to layer within a model in a pattern largely shared across models.

DeepSeek-R1
R1-Zero’s reasoning result and R1’s revised training procedure form one Nucleus because R1-Zero’s readability and language-mixing issues explain why the authors moved from one model to the other.

Development abstracts
=====================

Toy Models of Superposition
The phase change and polytope geometry are Evidence about how superposition behaves in the toy model, while the tentative link to adversarial examples is an Implication because it extends the explanation to another phenomenon.

Emergent Linear Representations
The discovery of a linear representation of the Othello board is the Nucleus, probing for the current player’s pieces versus the opponent’s is the Method, and controlling the model with vector arithmetic is an Implication because it is an application enabled by the central discovery of the paper.

Finding Structure in Time
Context sensitivity and generalization are Evidence because the simulations exhibit both, and the proposed application to lexical categories and the type/token distinction is an Implication attached to that Evidence because it depends on those two properties.

Datasheets for Datasets
The proposed datasheet and the information it should contain form one Nucleus because together they define the contribution, while better communication, transparency, and accountability are an Implication because they are expected benefits of adopting it.

Statistical Mechanics of Complex Networks
The final sentence is Scope because it names the empirical data, network models, and robustness questions the review covers, whereas Method would describe how the authors selected or analyzed that material.

The Information Bottleneck Method
The rate–distortion result is an Implication because the bottleneck formulation yields a distortion measure from the joint statistics of the input and target signals, showing how the formulation generalizes rate–distortion theory.

Final test abstracts
====================

Refusal in Language Models Is Mediated by a Single Direction
The Nucleus is the finding of one refusal-mediating direction per model, and the two interventions form a single Evidence planet because their opposite effects jointly support a causal role for that direction.

Observation of Gravitational Waves from a Binary Black Hole Merger
The final two sentences form one Nucleus because they state the discovery and what it establishes, while three Evidence planets support distinct parts of that claim: detection significance establishes the signal’s reliability, waveform agreement identifies a binary black hole merger, and the inferred masses support its stellar-mass classification.

Interaction Ruling Animal Collective Behaviour
The field estimate of six or seven interacting neighbors is Evidence for the topological rule, while the simulation result is Evidence attached to the Implication because it tests whether that rule helps preserve flock cohesion.

Power-law Distributions in Empirical Data
The synthetic tests and the 24 real datasets form separate Evidence planets because the first assess the framework under controlled conditions, while the second show which empirical power-law conjectures remain plausible when tested.

NDAI Agreements
The invention’s value and the agents’ reliability are separate Scope planets because one limits how much can be safely disclosed, while the other determines what safeguards are needed to preserve the gains.

The Capacity of Private Information Retrieval
The opening statement is Problem because it specifies what the user must retrieve and keep private, the capacity formula is the Nucleus because it gives the optimal download rate under those constraints, and the subset property is an Implication because the same scheme remains optimal when messages are removed.
