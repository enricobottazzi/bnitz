# Scientific Paper Structure Theory (SPST)

SPST is a BNITZ adaptation of Rhetorical Structure Theory (RST) for scientific papers. It borrows RST’s distinction between a central span (the nucleus) and attached spans, as well as its recursive structure. The nucleus is the paper’s main contribution. It can be a finding, theorem, argument, synthesis, artifact, guideline, or research plan. Each relation describes what an attached span does for its parent. [RST relation definitions](https://www.sfu.ca/rst/01intro/definitions.html)

## Ten relations

Positions follow a ten-hour clock. Position 10 is at the top, followed clockwise by 1 through 9.

| Relation | Function | Logo and position |
|---|---|---|
| **Problem–Response** | States the research question or practical problem the parent span addresses. | <img src="assets/problem-response.svg" alt="Ring with four inward leaves" width="32"> · 10 |
| **Background** | Gives context for understanding the parent span. | <img src="assets/circular-motif.svg" alt="Background motif" width="32"> · 9 |
| **Method** | Describes the procedures used to produce the contribution or planned for the research. | <img src="assets/method.svg" alt="Method motif" width="32"> (85) · 1 |
| **Evidence** | Gives observations or analyses that strengthen or weaken confidence in the parent claim. | <img src="assets/evidence.svg" alt="Evidence motif" width="32"> (207) · 6 |
| **Comparison** | Shows how the parent claim or contribution agrees with or differs from another. | <img src="assets/comparison.svg" alt="Two concentric rings" width="32"> (110) · 8 |
| **Explanation** | Proposes a cause or mechanism for what the parent span describes. | <img src="assets/explanation.svg" alt="Explanation motif" width="32"> (143) · 4 |
| **Interpretation** | Explains what the parent span means or what can be inferred from it, typically through qualitative reasoning. | <img src="assets/interpretation.svg" alt="Circular motif with a four-point ornament" width="32"> (213) · 3 |
| **Evaluation** | Assesses the parent claim or contribution against an explicit criterion, often using quantitative measures. | <img src="assets/evaluation.svg" alt="Evaluation motif" width="32"> · 7 |
| **Scope** | Specifies the conditions under which the parent claim or contribution applies. | <img src="assets/scope.svg" alt="Scope motif" width="32"> (170) · 2 |
| **Implication** | Draws a consequence for practice, prediction, or further research. | <img src="assets/implication.svg" alt="Six-petal rosette" width="32"> · 5 |

**Counterevidence** is a subtype of Evidence that weakens confidence in the span it attaches to. It shares position 6 with Evidence and has its own icon. <img src="assets/counterevidence.svg" alt="Outlined flower with a black center" width="32">

## Nesting

Attach each span to the part of the argument it directly supports, explains, or qualifies. A branch can have children of its own, and its relation to the parent covers the whole branch.
